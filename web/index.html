To play you need a deck of cards, a phone to hold above the table, a second screen and a Solana wallet on devnet.

HOW TO SET UP A TABLE
  1. Get a wallet. Install Phantom, Solflare or Backpack and switch it to Solana Devnet. Get free devnet SOL at faucet.solana.com.
  2. Open a table on a laptop or big screen. That screen is the dealer.
  3. Point the camera. Scan the camera QR with a phone and fix it about 1 m above the table, looking straight down.
  4. Check the areas. Under CAMERA & TOOLS, drag the board and seat areas to fit your table.
  5. Sit down. Each player scans a seat QR, buys in with devnet SOL and sits. Playing alone? You can add a bot under CAMERA & TOOLS.
  6. Play. Deal two cards each and press START ROUND. Bet on your phone and lay the board in the board area. After the river, put your two cards face up in your seat area. The winner is paid in CHIP, and you can cash out to SOL.
